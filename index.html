Privacy Policy

App: Sound Drop: Speaker Cleaner  |  Effective date: 17 November 2026

In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not need the internet to work.

This policy explains how the app "Sound Drop: Speaker Cleaner" ("the app"), published by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not send any data over the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (language, vibration on or off, auto-stop timer) only on your own device, so they are remembered the next time you open the app. This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: to vibrate the phone during a cleaning cycle if you keep vibration turned on.
  • Keep screen awake: so the screen does not turn off while a cleaning cycle is running.
  • The app plays sound through your speaker. It does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos or files.

4. Advertising and analytics

This version of the app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before the change is released.

5. Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

6. Using the app safely

The app plays low-frequency sounds that may help clear water or dust from a phone speaker. Results are not guaranteed. Keep the phone away from your ear while it is playing, and use a comfortable volume. If your phone has been in a lot of water, turn it off and let it dry fully.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the effective date shown above.
